## RepoSage

Clone a GitHub user's public repos and build a local, chunk-aware knowledge base to
answer questions about their code, with an LLM synthesizing answers cited back to
repo/file/line. Try it below, or bring your own public repo through the sandbox tab.

Source: [repo-sage on GitHub](https://github.com/AdrianHRedhe/repo-sage).
